Learn to read the order flow

A free 7-part course on auction theory and order flow — no magic signals, just the mechanics of how markets really move. Written by the builder of MATE.

CHAPTER 1

Price doesn't move. It auctions.

Auction theory: value, balance and imbalance — the foundation everything else is built on.

 CHAPTER 2

 CHAPTER 3

The footprint: the candle from the inside

 CHAPTER 4

The CVD: the delta that never forgets

The full session: every piece, one game

One session read step by step — context, liquidity, footprint, delta and CVD telling a single story.
