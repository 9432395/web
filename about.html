About us

Built by a trader who asked one question

When I started trading futures, I hit the same wall every retail trader knows: the tools that actually let you read the market — the footprint, the profiles, the order book — were locked behind subscriptions of $100, $200, even $300 a month. The message was clear: professional vision is for those who can pay professional prices.

And I kept asking myself the same question: why does software this important have to cost this much?

I had one advantage: I'm a software engineer. So instead of paying, I started building. First one tool for myself, then another, then another — each one tested candle by candle in the live market, rewritten until it was fast, honest and exact. What began as a personal toolbox slowly became something bigger: a complete professional suite.

Why MATE exists

MATE exists to prove that top-quality tools don't need elite prices. The entire suite — all 20 indicators — costs less than what others charge for a single module. Not because it's worth less, but because I believe reading the market well shouldn't be a luxury. The playing field is already tilted against the retail trader; your tools shouldn't tilt it further.

What I promise you

Honest software. Nothing here repaints, nothing predicts, nothing promises magic. Every number you see comes from real trades, classified tick by tick. If a value can't be measured honestly, my tools show you an N/A with its reason — never a decorated guess. That philosophy is engraved in every line of code.

And a real person on the other side. When you write to support, you're not talking to a ticket system in a giant company — you're talking to the person who wrote the code. Your suggestions don't go into a corporate backlog: the best ideas from users become features in the next update, included in your plan, always.
