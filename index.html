FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I receive the tools after purchase?

Right after payment you see your license key on screen and receive it by email with the download link. Installation is a standard NinjaTrader 8 import — under 5 minutes.

Does it work with my funded account or broker?

Yes. MATE tools run on any NinjaTrader 8 installation, with any broker or data provider.

On how many computers can I use my license?

Each license is personal and tied to your machine. Switching to a new computer? Write to support and we'll move it at no cost.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. One click starts your 7-day free trial — full access to the whole suite, no credit card required.

What does each plan include?

Every plan includes the complete suite with updates and support. The only difference is duration — the longer, the better the monthly price.

What market data do I need? Do I need Level 2?

Any data feed with tick data covers most of the suite. Only the Liquidity Heatmap needs market depth (Level 2) — check that your data subscription includes it. No Tick Replay is required.

Do I need a paid NinjaTrader license?

No. MATE runs on the free NinjaTrader 8 platform — charting and simulated trading included. You only ever pay NinjaTrader if you decide to trade live, and that is independent of MATE.

Which markets and instruments does it work on?

Any instrument in NinjaTrader 8 with real traded volume: futures (ES, NQ, CL, GC, micros…), stocks and more. Order flow shines brightest on liquid futures — that is where these tools were born.

How do I cancel? What happens when my subscription ends?

Cancel anytime from your Stripe receipt or by writing to support — no questions asked. You keep full access until the end of the period you already paid; after that, the tools simply stop loading. Full details in our Terms.
