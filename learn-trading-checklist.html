READ THE MARKET · SEASON 2 · FINAL CHAPTER

Professionals don't predict the day. They interrogate it.

Two seasons of concepts condense into one practical routine: six questions — three before the bell, three with the market live. This checklist is how an auction reader loads the map and keeps reading it.

Before the bell: 3 questions

One — where will the day open versus yesterday's value: inside, outside, gapping? Two — which references rule nearby: tails, poor extremes, naked POCs — the scars and pending appointments? Three — which game does the open hint at: range, trend, value migration? Hypotheses, not verdicts.

With the market open: 3 more

Four — acceptance or rejection? At every edge of value, the mother question: is price building business or coming right back? Five — who initiates and who responds? The location of every attack tells you the bet behind it. Six — does the rhythm confirm or break? Rotations and one-timeframing: the first clue the game changed.

The complete mental map

Season 1 gave you the tools that show what is happening: profiles and TPO, footprint, delta, CVD, the liquidity map. Season 2 gave you the theory that explains why it matters: value, acceptance, initiative, day types, rhythm, scars. Tools without theory are noise. Theory without tools is philosophy. Together, they are reading.

The honest rule: the auction is not predicted — it is interrogated. Six questions, asked every day, beat any magic signal ever sold.
