READ THE MARKET · SEASON 3 · FINAL CHAPTER

Your map, in ten minutes.

Everything this season taught condenses into a routine you can run before every open, in the time it takes to drink a coffee. Five steps before the bell, one after the first hour — and your chart carries the same references a professional desk loads every morning.

Before the bell: five marks

One — yesterday's value: VAH, VAL, POC. Where did the market last agree? Two — pending business: naked POCs and poor extremes within reach. Three — the night: overnight high, low, and where price sits inside that range — is inventory stretched? Four — the averages: session VWAP about to be born, weekly and monthly VWAP already on the chart. Five — the confluences: circle the zones where two or more of the above stack. That circle is where your attention will live today.

After the first hour: one more

Six — the Initial Balance: mark its high and low, note whether it printed wide or narrow, and from then on treat its edges like what they are — the day's first draft, waiting to be confirmed or rewritten by extensions and acceptance.

The trilogy, complete

Season 1 taught you to read the flow — what is happening right now, trade by trade. Season 2 taught you the auction — why it happens and what game the day is playing. Season 3 gave you the map — where it is likely to happen next. Flow, context, location: that is the complete reading. No predictions anywhere — just better questions, asked at better prices.

The honest rule: the map is preparation, not prophecy. Ten minutes of marks buys you a day of knowing where to look — what you do there is still decided by the auction, live.
