Lag Sentinel

The silent guardian of your trading: it watches the three pipes your money flows through — the data coming in, the orders going out and the platform painting it all.

3-PIPE WATCH SILENT UNTIL IT MATTERS

Is what you see on screen really the market, right now? Lag Sentinel answers that question honestly: it stays silent while everything is healthy, and when a pipe degrades, one line names the culprit. When it heals, it disappears.

Honest measurement, not theater

Cheap lag detectors print physically impossible numbers because they mix PC clock error with latency. Lag Sentinel measures deviation against your own session's baseline — immune to time zones and skewed clocks.

Data: live feed or frozen

It distinguishes what others confuse: a quiet tape is not a dead feed. It watches trades and quotes separately — quote silence is a frozen feed; trade silence with live quotes is just a quiet market.

Orders: the lag that costs money

It measures the real submit-to-acknowledge time of your own orders — it only listens, never touches them. Scalping with slow acknowledgements is paying a hidden spread; here you see it before you pay it.

Platform: when NT8 chokes

A background heartbeat detects when NinjaTrader's interface paints behind reality — too many charts, too many indicators. Your data may be fine; your picture of it, not.

Amber warns, red means stop

Amber background: something is slow — trade with margin. Red background, frozen feed: stop trusting the chart and manage risk from the broker. The color always reflects the worst pipe.

Robust statistics by construction

Medians and P95 over 60-second windows with hysteresis: a stray tick never screams, a sustained delay always does. And everything heals by itself, no intervention.
