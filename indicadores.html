Footprint Order Flow

The X-ray of every candle: who attacked, with how much size and at which exact price — with five layers of analysis built on tick-by-tick data.

TICK-EXACT NO REPAINTING

What you see, happened. Every number comes from individual trades classified tick by tick against the live bid/ask at the exact moment of each execution. Nothing interpolated, nothing smoothed, nothing repainted.

Delta and volume by price

Every candle broken down row by row: buys against the ask, sells against the bid, their net difference and total business. Delta and volume POCs marked in gold.

Diagonal imbalances

Automatic detection with the classic diagonal convention, configurable ratio and minimum-delta filter. Real imbalance, without false positives at the extremes.

Stacked & trapped zones

Stacked imbalances become living support and resistance zones — and when one fails, it is reborn as a trapped-traders zone: fuel for the other side.

Absorption and exhaustion

Smart signals computed at each candle close: huge volume with no delta at an extreme (absorption) or aggression running out of gas (exhaustion).

Unfinished auctions and Big Prints

Extremes where the auction didn't finish its business, and individual large executions, marked by side directly on the cell where they hit.

Value area and POC migration

VAH, VAL and per-candle value area with configurable percentage, plus the POC migration line connecting the center of gravity candle to candle.

Per-candle statistics table

Delta, delta change, delta %, total volume, imbalances and more — in three visual styles, with every row toggleable separately.

Real-time alerts

Zone touch, zone birth, absorption and exhaustion. All primed on connect: historical data never fires a false alarm.

Smart zoom

The numbers never change with zoom: the text adapts, compacts or yields to the cells — the footprint keeps speaking at any distance.

SETUP & READING
Requires

Historical tick data from your provider. No Tick Replay needed: it loads its own tick series and classifies against the recorded bid/ask (exact by default; Tick Replay available as an optional mode).

Key settings

Ticks Per Row, Display Mode (candle + footprint or footprint only), imbalance ratio & filter, alerts.

Reading the cells

Every row is one price. On the delta side, the left number is selling into the bid and the right number is buying into the ask. Imbalances compare diagonally — buys at one price vs sells one tick below, because that is how the ladder actually fights — and the dominant cell lights up. Three or more stacked imbalances become a zone that extends forward as support/resistance; when it breaks, it flips into a trapped-traders zone: fuel for the other side. A dot on a cell marks a single print at or above your Big Print threshold.

Chart setup

In Data Series, keep your candles small — 1 or 2 (minute, range or volume) — so the footprint stays readable. There is no universal Ticks Per Row: it depends on your instrument AND your candle size — the bigger the candle, the more rows it stacks, so raise Ticks Per Row until the cells read comfortably.

If you see nothing

Give the tick series a few seconds to load; on very long charts, reduce the days loaded.

Liquidity Heatmap

The side of the market the tape can't show: resting orders — where size waits, how it moves and what happens when price arrives.

REAL TIME FACTS ONLY

Raw facts only. No scores, no predictions, no repainting the past. Every episode ends in a measured fact — and every defense has its outcome: who won reads straight off the map.

Thermal map of the book

The history of every price painted as heat bands: hot = lots of resting size. The walls waiting for price, visible before it gets there.

Detected absorptions

A level that dies eating real volume gets marked on the map: someone fought there. The number inside tells you how many contracts it swallowed.

Icebergs uncovered

When a level executes more than it ever displayed, it was secretly replenishing. Green edge: the strongest fact this tool prints.

Pulled walls

Size that announces itself and leaves having executed almost nothing, recorded as a measured fact. When they pile up on one side, distrust that side.

Outcome of every defense

If price later crosses a defended level, its mark fades. The map tells you who won each battle — no interpretations.

Bookmap Mode + Volume Dots

Dedicated-platform thermal canvas with executed volume as dots by aggressor: where size rested, where it executed, who won.

DOM-style book panel

The live book anchored to the price axis: bars per level, best bid/ask with a white edge and open-spread warning. Numbers that never shrink.

One scale, the whole session

The same size produces the same color at 9:31 and at 15:59. The morning wall and the afternoon wall compare by eye, honestly.

Session persistence

Map, marks and outcomes are saved every minute: reload the chart and recover your session intact. A reload neither pardons nor condemns any mark.

SETUP & READING
Requires

A data feed with market depth (Level 2). The map builds in real time from the moment you connect; the past is restored from your own saved sessions (auto-saved every minute).

Key settings

Heatmap Max Reference (contracts = hottest color), Min Liquidity Threshold, Bookmap Mode, Max History (minutes).

Reading the marks

Filled chip = absorption (the number inside = contracts it swallowed) · hollow chip = pulled wall (the size that vanished) · border color = side (blue defended from below, red from above) · green border = iceberg · a mark that fades = that level was later broken.

If you see nothing

Check that your data subscription includes Level 2 — and remember the map starts painting from now, not from the past.

Session TPO & Volume Profile

A complete Market Profile station in a single indicator: the auction map in letters and real volume by price, side by side, session by session.

2 VIEWS IN 1 ZERO REPAINTING

Two truths, face to face. Where the market spent its time and where it committed its volume. When both agree, there's acceptance; when they diverge, there's information — and you see it before anyone else.

The classic TPO profile

The auction's alphabet letter by letter, with POC, value area, single prints, rejection tails and poor highs/lows — the complete grammar of Market Profile, automated.

Independent Volume Profile

Real traded volume by price with its own VPOC and its own value area, with separately configurable row resolution. Two lenses, one chart.

POC vs VPOC: the disagreement that informs

When the time-based and volume-based points of control diverge, the market is telling you something. Resolving that conflict is one of the finest reads in profile trading.

Merge and split with right-click

Merge several balance days into a composite profile and trade its edges; split a double-distribution day to see both auctions. Your view decisions survive reloads and restarts.

Five families of naked levels

Untested POCs, VPOCs, value areas and midpoints, extended forward until price trades through them — with tick-exact detection. The market's open questions, always in sight.

Initial Balance and markers

The initial range that frames the day's hypothesis, plus open and close markers for every session. The day's context, at a glance.

Continuous institutional zoom

Letters grow and shrink fluidly with the chart, and the profile widens under heavy zoom — the behavior of high-end desktop platforms.

Sessions your way

RTH, overnight, the chart's session or custom hours. Every profile respects your session definition, in your time zone.

Loads in seconds

Instant historical profiles or exact tick-by-tick reconstruction: you choose the balance between speed and absolute precision. Real time is always tick by tick.

SETUP & READING
Requires

Minute history (loads in seconds) or tick history (exact reconstruction) — you choose in the settings. Live is always tick-by-tick.

Key settings

Session (RTH / overnight / custom hours + time zone), TPO Chars, VP row resolution, historical profiles on/off.

Reading the letters

Each letter is one time bracket that visited that price (A the first, B the next…), so a long row means the market spent time there — acceptance. Singles — rows printed by a single bracket — mark where price crossed fast, without acceptance. A poor high/low, an extreme where two or more brackets stopped at the same row, is unfinished business that tends to get revisited. Naked POC/VPOC lines extend from past sessions until price finally touches them.

Right-click on a profile

Merge Session Right (build composites), Remove Merging, Split Session At Letter (cuts exactly at the letter under your cursor), Unsplit Session, and Split/Unsplit Letters. Your merges and splits persist — they survive reloads and restarts.

If profiles look off

Make sure the configured session matches your instrument's trading hours.

Big Trades

The institutional execution detector: every bubble on the chart is a large trade that actually happened — exact price, exact volume, aggressor identified.

SWEEP DETECTION WHAT YOU SEE, EXECUTED

The layer cheap detectors can't see. When an institution sends 127 contracts, the exchange reports them as 70+57 in the same instant — and simple detectors see two “small” trades and stay silent. Big Trades reconstructs the original order and draws it at its true size.

Reconstructed sweeps

Same instant, same aggressor side, price without pullback: the institutional order recomposed and drawn as a single event at the price where it really traded.

Bubbles that don't lie

Area proportional to volume, the exact number inside, color by aggressor side — and a golden ring marking the largest event in sight.

Accumulation levels

Prices where institutional size stacks up become lines of interest with their badge: “708 S54%” = 708 contracts, 54% sold. No ambiguity.

Big Delta

A CVD built only from institutional-size trades — often cleaner than the full delta. If price breaks out and Big Delta doesn't follow, size isn't participating.

Iceberg hunter in action

An iceberg's refills leave repeated prints and sweeps at the same price — exactly what accumulation levels bring to the surface.

Fresh session mode

Open the chart clean and turn every new bubble into an event, while statistics keep processing the full session underneath.

SETUP & READING
Requires

Tick data (own series; exact history from the recorded bid/ask — no Tick Replay needed).

Key settings

Print Threshold and tiers 2/3 — size them to YOUR instrument (big in ES is not big in NQ); Detect Sweeps and Sweep Window (ms).

Reading the bubbles

One bubble is one institutional execution at its true size — area proportional to volume, the exact number inside, color by aggressor side, and a golden ring on the largest event in sight. When big prints keep stacking at one price they build an accumulation level: its badge (“708 S54%”) reads 708 contracts, 54% of them sold — not just where size worked, but which side was doing it.

Too many / too few bubbles

Raise or lower the threshold — it's always the first knob.

Delta Suite

The aggression of every trade — who lifted the offer, who hit the bid — turned into three professional views in its own panel.

3 VIEWS IN 1 NO REPAINTING

The net number hides the battle. A candle closing at +50 after visiting −300 is not the same as a clean +50. Delta Suite's excursion wick shows you the absorption the final delta doesn't tell.

BarDelta with excursion wick

Each candle's net delta plus the intra-candle story: from the lowest to the highest point aggression visited. The whole battle, not just the result.

DeltaPct: the conviction view

+200 delta on 400 contracts is 50% dominance; on 4,000 it's a 5% stalemate. In climaxes, this view exposes the absorber.

CVD as candles or line

The session's cumulative delta as OHLC candles of accumulation. The underlying question, answered: who's in control, and whether that control is growing.

Divergences that never repaint

Price highs without CVD highs, marked on confirmed swings. A mark placed is a mark that doesn't move — ever.

Institutional lens

One switch rebuilds all three views using only large trades: if size's CVD disagrees with the total, you know what each crowd is doing.

Self-auditable to the contract

The per-candle total matches NinjaTrader's native volume exactly — check it yourself anytime. Data that shows its face.

SETUP & READING
Requires

Tick data. Add it as its own lower panel (not over price). Exact history by default (recorded bid/ask); Tick Replay optional.

Key settings

Mode (BarDelta / DeltaPct / CVD), Source (all volume or institutional only), Session (RTH/ETH), CVD style.

Reading the panel

Each histogram bar is the net aggression of that candle — positive, market buys dominated; Delta % normalizes it by volume so a quiet candle can still show conviction. The CVD is the running total: the trend of aggression. When price makes a new extreme and the CVD refuses to follow, that move carried less aggression than it appears — the classic divergence (optional marks included).

Trust check

The per-candle total matches NinjaTrader's native volume — compare them anytime.

Delta Profile

Who is winning the auction at every price, as a clean strip anchored to the chart's edge — with three ways to define “now”.

ROLLING MODE AGGRESSION MEMORY

Are you buying the push… or its echo? Rolling mode always contains exactly the last N minutes, and its freshness dots mark where aggression is happening right now — not the residue of a battle that already expired.

The auction by price

Aggressive delta per price row, with an intensity gradient and the max-delta row outlined in gold: the price where one side committed hardest.

Rolling: always the latest

A window that slides continuously, with no blind spot after a restart and no stale delta from hours ago. Data freshness guaranteed at every instant.

Freshness dots

White marks on the rows where aggression is genuinely recent — the difference between joining a live move and arriving late to a dead one.

DELTA vs PREV: the derivative

The card compares the current window with the previous one: +840 now versus +200 before = buyers accelerating. Opposite signs = a turn in progress.

Naked max-delta levels

Every closed period leaves its golden line where the aggressor prevailed — alive until price retests it, with tick-exact detection. Pure aggression memory.

The session's pulse

B 62% / S 38% at a glance: a 50/50 with a huge total is a battle; a 70/30 with a small total is an unopposed drift.

SETUP & READING
Requires

Tick data (own series).

Key settings

Anchor Mode (Session / Rolling / Reset), Rolling Minutes (your “now”: 30-60 min is typical), RTH/ETH hours, Placement (left or right edge).

Reading the strip

One horizontal bar per price at the chart's edge — blue, net buying won at that price; dark red, net selling won; the longer the bar, the bigger the net fight. It answers who is winning at each price inside the window you anchored — which is why Anchor Mode matters: Session is today's whole story, Rolling only your last N minutes.

If the strip starts empty

Rolling mode needs its first window minutes to fill.

Reading the profile

VWAP Suite

Five indicators, one standard: the daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly VWAP with deviation channels — the reference prices institutions measure their executions against.

5 INDICATORS TICK-EXACT

The VWAP that prints the same value on any chart. Most tools approximate it from candles: their value drifts and even changes with the timeframe. MATE's VWAPs are computed trade by trade — the true value, on any chart, always.

The five horizons

Daily (RTH and ETH separately), Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly and Yearly. From the intraday pulse to the anchor that position traders watch for months — the whole time hierarchy covered.

Up to five bands per VWAP

Deviation channels with per-band multiplier, colors and fill — in volume-weighted standard deviation or quarters of the true range, Market Profile style.

Prior Values: the period's memory

When each session, week, month or year closes, its VWAP and value channel are frozen and projected forward — the levels of the market's last agreement, ready for their first test.

Strictly individual periods

Each VWAP is born from zero at its open and dies at its close: no line ever crosses into the next period. No cross-session contamination, no false inheritances.

Institutional anchoring

Weeks, months, quarters and years anchored to the trading day — the professionals' convention. Holidays, shortened sessions and data gaps can't move the anchor.

Your session, your time zone

Configurable session hours interpreted in the zone you choose, with automatic daylight saving. 09:30 New York is the open, wherever you are.

SETUP & READING
Requires

Tick data (computed trade by trade — the same value on any chart).

Key settings

Each horizon is its own indicator (add Daily, Weekly… the ones you use); session hours + time zone; band multipliers; Prior Values for the frozen levels of the last period.

Reading the lines

The center line is the average price actually paid in the session, weighted by volume — the market's running fair price. The bands measure how stretched price is from that average, in deviations or quarter-range. Prior Values freeze the previous period's VWAP and bands on today's chart: reference levels many desks benchmark against, which is why they tend to get tested.

If the value looks “off”

Check session hours and time zone — a VWAP is only as good as its session definition.

Lag Sentinel

The silent guardian of your trading: it watches the three pipes your money flows through — the data coming in, the orders going out and the platform painting it all.

3-PIPE WATCH SILENT UNTIL IT MATTERS

Is what you see on screen really the market, right now? Lag Sentinel answers that question honestly: it stays silent while everything is healthy, and when a pipe degrades, one line names the culprit. When it heals, it disappears.

Honest measurement, not theater

Cheap lag detectors print physically impossible numbers because they mix PC clock error with latency. Lag Sentinel measures deviation against your own session's baseline — immune to time zones and skewed clocks.

Data: live feed or frozen

It distinguishes what others confuse: a quiet tape is not a dead feed. It watches trades and quotes separately — quote silence is a frozen feed; trade silence with live quotes is just a quiet market.

Orders: the lag that costs money

It measures the real submit-to-acknowledge time of your own orders — it only listens, never touches them. Scalping with slow acknowledgements is paying a hidden spread; here you see it before you pay it.

Platform: when NT8 chokes

A background heartbeat detects when NinjaTrader's interface paints behind reality — too many charts, too many indicators. Your data may be fine; your picture of it, not.

Amber warns, red means stop

Amber background: something is slow — trade with margin. Red background, frozen feed: stop trusting the chart and manage risk from the broker. The color always reflects the worst pipe.

Robust statistics by construction

Medians and P95 over 60-second windows with hysteresis: a stray tick never screams, a sustained delay always does. And everything heals by itself, no intervention.

SETUP & READING
Requires

Nothing special. Add it and forget it: silence means everything is healthy.

Key settings (optional)

Warning thresholds and text position. The defaults are tuned to never cry wolf.

Reading the readout

A small text line on your chart — gray on black, everything healthy; amber, a sustained warning above your thresholds; dark red, a real problem. It watches three things by pure listening: your data feed's delay, your order round-trip (submit to ack) and NinjaTrader's own UI thread. It never touches your orders — and if you never notice it, everything is fine.

Auction Context

A single panel answering, before and during every session, the question every auction trader asks: how big is today compared to a normal day?

CONTEXT IN ONE PANEL NO DEGRADED VALUES

The pre-open in ten seconds. Ranges against their typical, relative volume by clock minute, overnight inventory and rotations — every number is a real measurement of your own instrument, and the developing day never contaminates its own averages. Honest context, not magic signals.

Ranges against normal

Overnight, Initial Balance, RTH and ETH measured against their N-day typical. One percentage and you know: today is compressed, normal or extended — and you adjust targets instead of trading every day the same.

RVOL by clock minute

Your cumulative volume compared against the typical cumulative at this exact minute of the session — because comparing 10:00 against a full-day average means nothing.

Overnight inventory

How much of the night traded above or below settlement: who arrives loaded at the open. A 100% one-sided night is information the first hour will put to the test.

Exact delta, not estimated

Overnight and session delta classified tick by tick against the bid/ask recorded with each trade — the overnight frozen at the open, the day's accumulated live.

Rotation calculator

Three parallel detectors with thresholds that adapt to current volatility: your instrument's natural swing unit, right now. And the current leg measured against the average — routine rotation or something bigger?

Radical honesty with data

Every value is a real measurement or an explicit N/A with its reason — never a silently degraded number. Averages are fed only with complete, closed sessions.

SETUP & READING
Requires

Times are interpreted in ET (New York), or use your Trading Hours template. Loads its own minute + tick series.

Key settings

Lookback (days) — the “normal” it compares against; Initial Balance (minutes); median vs mean; panel position.

Reading the panel

Each row compares today against your instrument's own normal — current value, typical value and percent of typical — built from your lookback (median or mean, your choice). That turns raw numbers into context: an Initial Balance at 150% of typical is a very different day from one at 60%.

If you see N/A

That's deliberate — not enough complete sessions yet for an honest average.
